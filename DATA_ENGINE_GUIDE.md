
# DTS Quant Bot — Data Engine

## Purpose
The data engine prepares historical market data for quantitative research.

## Responsibilities
- Load historical CSV and Parquet files.
- Validate timestamps and OHLCV fields.
- Detect missing or invalid data.
- Prepare clean datasets for backtesting.
- Record data-quality results.

## Safety
- Research and paper trading only.
- Maximum planned risk per trade: 1%.
- No live order execution.
